# Discord Research Output

Use this order for the initial deep-research response. Omit an empty subsection rather than inventing content, but keep every top-level heading.

## 핵심 요약

- Identify the work or video's central significance in 3-6 bullets.
- Distinguish verified facts from Hermes analysis.
- State transcript or access limitations here when they materially affect confidence.

## 조사 기록

Keep this section in the same Discord thread as the analysis. It is a compact, reader-facing research record—not hidden chain-of-thought and not a raw tool output dump.

### 확인한 자료

- Group the material actually retrieved and read by source class: video/official metadata, transcript status, official or primary material, editorial review, post-release interview or 제작기, and community reception.
- For each material source, record the publication/platform, date when available, what was checked, the specific finding or short evidence-bearing quotation, and its inline citation.
- Distinguish a full-page retrieval from a search-result lead. A lead may identify a next step but cannot support an asserted finding.

### 제외·접근 실패

- Record material dead ends that affect confidence: deleted/private/region-blocked pages, login walls, unavailable transcripts, paywalled pages, duplicate syndications, and review or community leads whose original page could not be read.
- State the reason the item was excluded and what was or was not inferred from it. Do not turn inaccessible snippets into evidence.

### 커뮤니티 탐색 기록

- List the attempted, accessible, and unavailable community platforms/spaces, the relevant query dimensions used, the collection time window, and the stopping reason.
- This must preserve the investigation scope without exposing raw tool output, private data, usernames, internal scratch notes, or chain-of-thought.

## 리뷰와 후일담

This is a high-priority section. Research it before expanding the work analysis.

### 매체별 리뷰 요약

- Find individual editorial reviews through Album of the Year and similar review-discovery sites, then retrieve and read the original publisher page whenever it is accessible.
- Treat Album of the Year, Metacritic-style aggregators, user scores, rating distributions, and search snippets as discovery or aggregation evidence—not as an editorial review by themselves. 집계 점수는 개별 평론을 대체하지 않는다.
- For every 직접 읽은 개별 리뷰, provide one compact entry: **publication, date, score** (only if the page explicitly publishes one), core verdict, 2-4 distinctive observations, and an inline citation.
- Name the outlet and preserve the original review title or a short original-language phrase when useful. Do not flatten different critics into one generic “reviews were positive/mixed” sentence.
- If the original review page is inaccessible, label that outlet as an aggregator-discovered lead; do not summarize its alleged argument or score as if the full review was read.
- Separate editorial criticism from user/community reception. Report aggregate figures only as labeled context, with their methodology and date when available.

### 후일담·인터뷰·제작기

- Prioritize post-release interviews, making-of coverage, studio/production reports, official behind-the-scenes material, creative statements, and later reporting about reception, controversy, touring, revisions, or collaborators.
- For each materially useful article, explain what happened, who said it, when it was published, and why it changes or sharpens the reading of the work.
- Translate the relevant factual passage or a short quotation into Korean, preserving the original-language quote alongside it when useful. Mark the Korean 번역 as Hermes translation.
- Do not translate or reproduce a full review, interview, article, transcript, lyrics, or other copyrighted text. Summarize it in Korean and quote only brief excerpts needed for analysis.
- Keep the report date explicit: later coverage must not be presented as if it were part of the original release context.

- Search 공개 게시물 about the canonical artist, work, album, or video on Reddit, DCInside, HIPHOPLE, and other materially relevant music communities. Use a platform's public and accessible pages only; do not bypass login walls, access private communities, or collect personal information.

Reddit, DCInside, HIPHOPLE는 시작점일 뿐이며 관련 공개 커뮤니티 목록을 고정하지 않는다.

Seek materially different communities rather than many near-duplicate threads from one platform; continue broad discovery until information saturation, then state the coverage boundary instead of claiming exhaustiveness.

## 작품·영상 분석

For music, cover the composition or recording, lyrics or themes, structure, genre, sound, performance, credits, and visual direction where evidence permits.

For general video, cover the thesis, structure, evidence, participants, production choices, and limitations.

Use short quotations only. Add verified timestamps for transcript quotations.

## 배경과 맥락

Explain the artist or creator, album or series, release history, period, cultural setting, precedents, reception, influence, and connected works. Keep later interpretation separate from contemporary evidence.

## 검증 정보

Present important credits, dates, versions, release data, and other checkable details. When sources conflict, show both accounts with separate citations and explain the confidence judgment.

## 관련 작품과 다음 탐색

Recommend related recordings, videos, samples, covers, interviews, books, articles, or archives. Explain each connection instead of giving an unannotated list.

## 출처와 불확실성

Include:

1. a compact list of unresolved or weakly supported points
2. verified transcript language metadata, `language not verified`, or a statement that the transcript is unavailable
3. the mechanically rendered numbered source list
4. a one-line completion note

Citation rules:

- Place inline numbered citations immediately after the sentence they support.
- Use no more than three citations on one sentence.
- Use `[unverified]` only when a material claim genuinely cannot be sourced.
- Never hand-type or invent a URL in the final source list.
